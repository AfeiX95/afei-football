# 9800X3D + RTX 5060 Ti Build: Component Recommendations

*Research date: July 2026 | Focus: Price-performance, Chinese market*

---

## 1. PSU (Power Supply)

### Recommended: **750W 80+ Gold** — plenty; **850W** for futureproofing

| Component | Typical Gaming Power Draw |
|---|---|
| Ryzen 7 9800X3D | 80–120W |
| RTX 5060 Ti | ~150–180W (est.) |
| Rest of system | ~50–80W |
| **Total system** | **~300–380W gaming** |
| **Safe recommendation** | **750W** (2× headroom) |

**MSI PSU table** officially recommends:
- RTX 5060 Ti Standard = **600W**
- RTX 5060 Ti OC = **650W** (R5) / **750W** (R7/9800X3D)

**Common sense**: 750W is more than enough for this combo. 850W gives room for future GPU upgrades and keeps the PSU in its efficiency sweet spot.

**Top price-performance picks (Chinese market):**
- **Cooler Master MWE Gold 750 V2** (~¥450-550) — solid, reliable, fully modular
- **Great Wall (长城) G7 750W** (~¥400-500) — great value in Chinese market
- **Thermaltake Toughpower GF1 750W** (~¥500-600)
- **Vetroo 850W 80+ Gold** (~¥400-500) if going 850W
- **Seasonic Focus GX-750** (~¥600-700) — premium pick

## 3. DDR5 RAM

### Sweet spot: **DDR5-6000 CL30** (32GB = 2×16GB)

AMD's Infinity Fabric runs 1:1 with memory at 6000 MT/s. Going above 6000 causes the interconnect to drop to 2:1 mode, yielding **diminishing or zero gaming gains**.

| Speed | AMD Performance | Price | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DDR5-6000 CL30** ⭐ | Optimal (1:1 FCLK) | Moderate | **Best for gaming** |
| DDR5-6400 CL32 | Marginal gain on Intel only | Higher | Not worth it on AMD |
| DDR5-7200+ | Diminishing returns on AMD | Much higher | Only for enthusiasts |
| DDR5-5600 CL46 | Works but slower | Low | Budget option only |

### Key takeaway
- **750W PSU** is sufficient; **850W** is nice-to-have futureproofing
- **Air cooling** (Phantom Spirit 120 SE) is all the 9800X3D needs — skip the AIO to save ¥200+
- **DDR5-6000 CL30** is the RAM sweet spot for Ryzen 9000 series — anything faster gives negligible gains
- **Mid-tower ATX case** with mesh front for airflow — Montech XR gives the best price-performance
